Emergency sewer line cleaning in Coconut Creek, FL

Sewer line cleaning is the removal of a blockage from the main drain that carries all of your home's wastewater out to the sewer. In Coconut Creek, FL, emergency sewer line cleaning is what you need when a toilet, tub and floor drain all fail at once, because that pattern means the stoppage is in the main line and not in one fixture.

We answer 24/7, and a technician can often arrive the same day, including after hours and on weekends. Stop using water as soon as you suspect a main line stoppage, since every flush adds to the pile.

Sewage backing up: what to do first

This is the order that protects your home while the crew is on the way.

1

Stop the water

No flushing, no laundry, no showers. If the dishwasher is running, cancel the cycle.

2

Find the lowest drain

A floor drain, shower or tub on the ground floor usually overflows first. Put towels or a bucket there and keep the area clear.

3

Keep contact to a minimum

Sewage carries bacteria. Wear gloves and boots, wash well afterward and keep pets and children out of the room.

4

Locate the cleanout

It is usually a capped pipe in the yard or near the house wall. Do not open it unless we ask. It helps us to know where it is.

Signs of a main line stoppage

A blocked main line has a recognizable pattern, which helps us arrive with the right machine.

More than one drain is slow

Bathrooms, kitchen and laundry all drain badly at the same time.

Toilet flush moves water in the tub

Water or bubbles appear in a shower or tub when you flush, because the air and water have nowhere else to go.

Ground-floor drains overflow

The lowest opening in the system gives way first.

Gurgling from the pipes

Air trapped behind the blockage escapes through traps with a gurgling sound.

Sewage smell inside or out

A smell near the cleanout or in the yard can mean standing wastewater.

Cable or jet: how the main line gets cleared

We pick the method after hearing the symptoms and checking the line. These are the two main options.

Cable machine

  • The standard first response for a stopped main line.
  • A cutter head chews through paper, soft debris and many root masses.
  • Fast to set up from a cleanout.
  • May leave grease on the pipe wall.

Water jet

  • Used when buildup keeps coming back after cabling, see hydro jetting.
  • Washes the pipe wall for a longer-lasting result.
  • Used on a pipe a camera shows is sound.
  • Takes more setup than a cable.

Why we camera the line after cleaning

A line that is clear is not the same as a line that is healthy. After the stoppage is cleared, a camera shows whether it was a one-time clog, a root mass at a joint, a sagging section that holds water or a crack.

That footage turns a guess into a plan. A one-time clog needs nothing more, a root-filled joint points to sewer line repair, and a mystery repeat backup is a reason to book a full sewer camera inspection.

Local factors for main line stoppages in Coconut Creek

Where you live changes what we ask first.

Who owns which pipe

The City of Coconut Creek maintains local sewer infrastructure and Broward County treats the wastewater. If neighbors on your street are also backed up, the blockage may be in the public main, so call your utility too.

Margate side of town

Part of the area south of Coconut Creek Parkway is served by the City of Margate. Its Department of Environmental and Engineering Services takes sewer stoppage calls at 954-972-0828, which is worth having if the problem is outside your property.

Storm season

The Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30. After a heavy storm, a backup that appears in several homes at once is usually a utility question, and a backup in only your home is ours to clear.

Older neighborhoods

City planning documents describe South Creek as more than 50 years old as of 2019. Lines of that age can have roots and rough joints, so repeat stoppages there deserve a camera look.

How a sewer line cleaning visit goes

  1. 1

    Find the access point

    We look for your cleanout. When there is none, we enter through a toilet or a roof vent.

  2. 2

    Clear the stoppage

    A cable machine is fed in until the blockage breaks and water moves.

  3. 3

    Flush and test

    We run water through the line and watch the flow at the cleanout.

  4. 4

    Camera and report

    A camera pass shows the pipe, and you hear what we saw before any repair talk begins.

What affects the price of sewer line cleaning

The price follows the job, not a flat rate. These are the pieces that matter.

Cleanout access

An outside cleanout means a quick setup, while entering through a toilet takes longer.

Distance to the blockage

A stoppage near the house is simpler than one far down the lateral.

Cause

Paper and grease clear easily, but roots and a broken pipe take more work.

Equipment

A cable machine and a water jet are different tools with different time needs.

Camera follow-up

The inspection after cleaning is a separate step you can add or skip.

A sewer backup that damages floors or walls may fall under a sewer backup endorsement, which not every policy has. Check your policy and keep your photos and the invoice.
