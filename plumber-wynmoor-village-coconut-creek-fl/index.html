Wynmoor Village in Coconut Creek

The city's parks plan calls Wynmoor Village the largest active adult community in Coconut Creek, with 5,244 units, and says it was about 40 years old as of 2019. Real-estate sites describe it as a gated 55-plus community of mid-rise condo buildings grouped into villages.

The Township is a separate all-ages community, so say Wynmoor when you call. For plumbing elsewhere in the city, see our Coconut Creek emergency plumbing page.

Plumbing calls that fit Wynmoor condos

Inside a unit, most calls are the same as in any home. The difference is knowing where your responsibility ends.

  • A stain on a ceiling or wall, or a neighbor reporting water, which starts with leak detection
  • A tank that leaks or no longer heats, handled through water heater replacement or repair
  • A toilet that runs or leaks at the base, covered by toilet repair
  • A dripping faucet or a failed shutoff under a sink, fixed with faucet repair
  • A sink or tub that drains slowly, which is a job for drain cleaning if only your unit is affected

Before you call a plumber in a Wynmoor condo

Check your association documents first, or call the office, to learn who repairs which pipes. Water inside your unit, such as a fixture, a heater or the line behind your own wall, is usually the owner's side. Shared stacks and risers are usually the association's, but your documents decide.

Tell us the village, building number and unit when you call, and whether the entrance is gated, so the technician can get in. If water is reaching a unit below, shut off your unit's water if you can reach the valve, and tell the association right away.

You can verify any plumber on the state's Verify a Licensee page at myfloridalicense.com. Our service areas page lists the other towns we serve.
