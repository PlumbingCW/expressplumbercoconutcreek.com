What counts as a plumbing emergency in Coconut Creek

Call the emergency line for anything that is spreading, flooding or leaving you without water or hot water. Examples are a burst pipe, sewage backing into a tub or floor drain, a leaking tank, or a meter that will not stop turning after you close every tap.

An emergency plumber visit starts with stopping the damage. We help you find the main shutoff over the phone, then dispatch a plumber to locate the cause and fix it or make it safe. A slow drain or a dripping faucet can wait for a scheduled visit, and we will say so honestly when you call.

Here is how callers usually describe emergencies, and where each one is handled. A clog that backs up more than one fixture is a job for drain cleaning or sewer line cleaning. A wet ceiling or a running meter belongs to leak detection. A cold shower and a puddle by the tank is a water heater repair call, and a warm spot on the floor can mean a slab leak.

Plumbing in Coconut Creek: what is specific to this city

Coconut Creek spans about 12.78 square miles, and the line between its two water utilities runs along Coconut Creek Parkway. The City of Coconut Creek utility serves homes north of the Parkway, and the City of Margate utility serves homes south of it. Knowing which one sends your bill tells you who to call about a meter, a bill or a main break outside your property line.

Communities such as South Creek, Wynmoor, the Township and Centura Park were built in different decades. City planning documents describe South Creek as more than 50 years old and Wynmoor as about 40 years old as of 2019. Older homes tend to show their age in supply lines and water heaters, so a repiping or leak detection visit is worth asking about if you see rust-colored water or low pressure.

We serve addresses along Sample Road, Lyons Road, Hillsboro Boulevard and the Coconut Creek Parkway corridor, from the area around Tradewinds Park and Butterfly World to the MainStreet district and The Promenade at Coconut Creek. Our line covers ZIP codes 33063, 33066 and 33073, and the 33097 PO box ZIP, for leak detection, drain cleaning and water heater service.
