Coral Springs plumbing problems we get called for first

Dispatch runs around the clock for Coral Springs. Most calls start with a symptom rather than a service name, and these are the ones that send a plumber out fast.

Every drain slows at once

When tubs, sinks and the washer all drain slowly together, the blockage is usually in the main line, not a single trap.

Gurgling and bubbling

Air pushed back through a toilet or tub drain when another fixture runs points to a restricted line downstream.

Sewage in the lowest fixture

Water rising in a shower or floor drain when you flush is a main line backup. Stop using water.

A recurring clog

The same drain stopping every few months suggests roots, a bellied pipe or buildup that snaking only clears for a while.

No hot water

A cold shower on a morning with no other warning is a water heater problem.

A wet ceiling or a running meter

Both call for leak detection before anything gets opened.

A fully built-out city and what that means for drain lines

Coral Springs was planned from the start, and the city says it is fully built-out. Its own housing counts run from 28,855 units in 1990 to 41,810 in 2000, so most of the city's homes date to before 2000.

Pipe that old carries decades of grease, soap scale and tree roots in the line between the house and the sewer connection. We run a camera through the line first, because it shows a cracked section, a sag or a root mass that a plain snake would only punch through.

Once we can see the problem, the repair choices are clear. A short clog gets drain cleaning, a heavy buildup may call for hydro jetting, and a damaged section is a sewer line repair conversation with the camera footage on the table.

Four water providers, so call the right one first

Coral Springs is unusual in that four separate providers serve different parts of the city. We cannot tell from a street name which one serves you, so start with your bill.

  • City of Coral Springs Water District, 954-344-1825, serves Central and Northeast Coral Springs from the Sawgrass Expressway to State Road 7.
  • Coral Springs Improvement District, 954-753-0380.
  • North Springs Improvement District, 954-752-0400.
  • Royal Water Works, 888-228-2134.
  • The city offers a blockage check in the city right-of-way at 954-345-2160 for City water district customers only. If the blockage is on the city side, our plumber cost is not reimbursed, so use that call before you pay for a camera when your whole street is backing up.

Where the stoppage sits decides who fixes it

The same symptom can sit in two different places. A quick test narrows it before we dig or cut.

On your property (call us)

  • One fixture slow, so likely a trap or branch line a cable usually clears.
  • Several fixtures slow or backing up, so likely the main line, and a camera shows roots, grease or a break.
  • Sewage in the lowest drain while neighbors are fine.

In the utility's pipe (call your provider)

  • Your whole street backing up, with neighbors affected too.
  • A backup the camera shows sitting beyond your property line.
  • A blockage the city check at 954-345-2160 finds in the right-of-way.

Before we arrive at your Coral Springs address

A licensed plumber is on the way from Coconut Creek, roughly 12 to 18 minutes by Sample Road under normal traffic. Until the truck arrives, take these steps.

1

Stop the water going in

Do not flush, run the dishwasher or start the washer. Every gallon you send down adds to the backup.

2

Find the cleanout

Look for a capped 3 or 4 inch pipe near the house or the property line. Do not open it if water is already standing above the cap.

3

Move people and pets

Sewage carries bacteria. Keep children, pets and bare feet away from the affected room.

4

Tell us the first symptom

Which drain slowed first, and has anyone used the toilet since? That points us to the right section of line.
