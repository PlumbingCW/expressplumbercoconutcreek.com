Hurricane season and Deerfield Beach plumbing

The Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30. Deerfield Beach's boil-water page notes that precautionary notices may follow a hurricane or tropical storm and a loss of power or pressure.

After a storm, check for a sewer backup, a heater that will not relight and wet ceilings, since all three follow power and pressure loss. If a boil notice is posted, ask the city before using tap water, and call us for the pipes and fixtures.

Before the season starts, a sewer camera inspection shows whether the line can take a surge of water. We do not promise a storm will leave you dry, but a line you have seen is a line you can plan around.

Sewer and drain trouble in an older housing stock

The city's own housing plan states that "much of Deerfield Beach housing problems are as a result of the age of the housing stock." The city counts about 42,671 housing units, so older homes are a real share of what we see.

In older pipe, a main line stoppage usually points to roots, a bellied section or years of grease. We clear the line, then run a camera so you see the cause. If the camera shows damage, sewer line repair comes next, with the footage and a clear price.

City crews handle the sewer system, so a whole-street backup is a city call. A single house backing up with the neighbors fine is our kind of job.

Water heaters and the city water supply

The City of Deerfield Beach runs its own water supply from the Biscayne and Floridan aquifers, using 14 wells, and disinfects with chloramination. It uses quicklime softening to reduce hardness, and its report gives no hardness number we can quote.

Chloramine can age rubber seals and flappers faster than plain chlorine, which is a general trade point worth knowing when a toilet starts running. A water heater is where scale and sediment collect, and a water heater repair visit covers both.

The city also runs an annual free chlorination flush of its distribution system. If water looks or tastes different, ask the city whether a flush is underway before you call a plumber.
