Older Margate homes and what decades of service do to plumbing

The City of Margate says it grew rapidly for several decades after a farmland purchase in 1953, starting with Hammon Heights east of State Road 7. It became a town in 1955 and a city in 1961, so many Margate houses have had decades of daily water use behind their walls.

We do not guess what is inside a wall from a street name. The first visit checks for worn supply lines with pinhole leaks, a tired water heater and drains that have slowed, and then we price the real problem.

If you own an older Margate home and see a stain, a climbing bill or weak pressure, start with a meter check and a pressure test. That one visit separates a spot repair from a repiping job to plan.

Margate's water utility and where your side of the meter begins

The City of Margate owns and operates its own water and wastewater systems, with East and West plants. The same utility serves the part of Coconut Creek south of Coconut Creek Parkway, so a Coconut Creek neighbor may share your bill and your phone number for main breaks.

Margate draws from the Biscayne Aquifer, uses lime softening to reduce hardness and disinfects with chloramine. The city report gives us no hardness figure to quote, so read its latest water quality report before you shop for a softener.

The city also lists a five-year programme to replace water lines. If pressure drops across a whole street, that is a utility matter. If only your house is affected, the cause is usually on your side of the meter, and that is when to call us.

Before we arrive at your Margate address

A licensed plumber is leaving our Coconut Creek base on Sample Road. By road the trip is about 4 to 5 miles, roughly 10 to 15 minutes, and traffic can change that.

1

Close the shutoff and drain the line

Turn the main valve at the house wall or meter box fully off, then open one faucet to release pressure. If the valve is seized, tell dispatch so the technician brings a replacement.

2

Keep power away from water

Switch off breakers only for rooms with standing water, and only if you can reach the panel without stepping through any.

3

Stop using drains

If sewage is coming up, stop flushing and running sinks, and keep children and pets out of the overflow.

4

Gather three details

Note the nearest cross street, how many fixtures are affected and where your meter box sits. Dispatch uses all three.
