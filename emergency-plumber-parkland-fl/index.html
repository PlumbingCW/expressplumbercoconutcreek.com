Three utilities and no city system, so your community name matters

Parkland does not run its own water system. The City of Coconut Creek utility, North Springs Improvement District and Parkland Utilities, Inc. each serve different communities, so the right first call depends on where you live.

The City of Coconut Creek utility (954-973-6732) serves County Point, Country Place, Cypress Trail, Sable Pass, Mayfair, Mill Run, Parkland Terraces, Lakes at Parkland, Parkland Place, Shoppes of Parkland and Whittier Oaks, among others. That is the same utility that bills our home city, so we know it well.

Tell us your community when you call. It decides whether a pressure drop or a discolored tap is a utility matter or something inside your house.

Test your tap before you buy a softener or a filter

We do not state a hardness figure for any Parkland utility, because none is published for the private one and we will not guess. The honest way to find out is to test your tap, either with a test kit or a lab test, before you spend money on equipment.

The City of Parkland notes that residents of the private utility have raised water-quality complaints, and the state says the system meets minimum standards. If your water smells, stains fixtures or leaves scale on the heater, a test points to the right fix, whether that is a water softener or filtration system or nothing at all.

Scale on a heating element or sediment in the tank also shortens heater life, so a water heater repair visit is a good moment to ask for a test.

Wells, septic and the parts of Parkland that run differently

The City of Parkland lists a contact for wells and septic tanks, so some properties here do not use a standard sewer connection. The city does not say where, so the first visit confirms what you have before we plan any work.

Septic work is a different job from a sewer backup. A drain that slows only when the whole system is full may be a septic issue, and in that case we tell you plainly if the next call belongs to a septic specialist.

The city also notes that repairing faucets and valves does not need a permit, and fixture replacement may. We do not promise what applies to your job, so ask the Parkland Building Department at 954-753-5447 before a larger project.

Before we arrive at your Parkland address

Longer trips need a better handoff, so give dispatch these four details and then work through the list. The drive from Coconut Creek is about 15 to 25 minutes, depending on the roads.

1

Name your community

Tell us which Parkland community you live in so we know which utility serves you.

2

Shut off the heater supply

If the tank is leaking, close the cold water valve above it. If it is electric, switch off its breaker before touching anything near the water.

3

Close the main

Shut the main valve at the house if water is spraying or pooling and cannot be stopped.

4

Skip hot taps

Do not run hot water if the heater is leaking or making noise. Scalding steam can escape.
