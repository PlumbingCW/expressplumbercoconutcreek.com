Emergency burst pipe repair in Coconut Creek, FL

Burst pipe repair means stopping the water at a split, cracked or separated pipe, cutting out the failed section and joining in new material that matches the rest of the line. In Coconut Creek, FL, emergency burst pipe repair covers supply lines spraying in a wall or ceiling, a cracked fitting under a sink, and a broken pipe that shows up as a soaked floor.

Our line answers 24/7. We offer same-day visits when the schedule allows, and after-hours calls are handled the same way as daytime ones. Shut the water off first, then call us while the house dries out.

The first 10 minutes after a pipe bursts

Water causes more damage each minute it runs. Work through these steps before the plumber arrives.

1

Close the main water shutoff

Turn the main valve clockwise to stop all supply to the house. It is often at the meter box near the street or on the wall where the supply enters the house. A stuck valve can break, so turn it steadily and not with force.

2

Turn off the power near wet areas

If water is reaching outlets, light fixtures or the electrical panel, switch off those breakers only if the panel itself is dry and you can reach it safely. Otherwise stay clear and call an electrician or the power utility.

3

Open faucets to drain the lines

With the main closed, open a low and a high faucet to let the pipes empty. This reduces how much water is left to leak at the break.

4

Move belongings and photograph the damage

Lift rugs, electronics and boxes off the floor. Photograph the wet walls, ceilings and the broken pipe if you can see it. Keep these for your insurer.

If the water is coming from the heater closet instead, check our water heater repair page, since a failed tank looks a lot like a burst pipe.

Signs you have a cracked or broken pipe

Not every burst is a geyser. Most callers notice something small first.

Water spraying or pouring from a wall or ceiling

A pressurized supply line has split. Shut the main at once.

A hissing sound with no fixture running

A pinhole or crack under pressure often hisses before it becomes visible.

Low water pressure everywhere

Water is escaping upstream of your fixtures, often at a fitting or a crack.

A wet patch on a slab floor or warm spot underfoot

A leaking hot line can warm the concrete above it. Call before the flooring lifts.

Stained, bubbling or sagging drywall

Water has been traveling behind the surface, sometimes for days.

A water meter that moves with everything off

A steady spin points to a leak between the meter and your fixtures.

How we find the break

When the leak is plain to see, repair starts as soon as the water is off. When it is behind drywall or under a slab, we narrow it down with a pressure test, acoustic listening equipment and, where useful, a thermal camera that shows a warm hot-water line against a cooler floor.

The goal is to open as small a section of wall or floor as possible. If the source is still unclear after our first checks, our leak detection service traces it before anything is cut.

Repairing copper, PEX and CPVC lines

The fix depends on the pipe material and how it failed. We identify the material first, then choose a repair that suits it.

Copper pipe repair

Copper splits, pinholes and corroded elbows are cut out and replaced with a new length of copper or a transition to another material. Joints are cleaned and soldered or fitted with press connectors. If several pinholes show up along one run, the pipe itself may be wearing out and we tell you.

PEX pipe repair

PEX is flexible and tolerates movement, but fittings, crimp rings and sun-damaged runs can fail. A damaged section is cut out and replaced with new tubing and approved connectors. PEX left in direct sunlight outdoors degrades, so exposed runs get covered or rerouted.

CPVC pipe repair

CPVC becomes brittle with age and heat, and cracks often show at fittings or bends. We replace the failed section with new pipe and solvent-welded joints, then check nearby fittings that may be in the same condition.

Drain pipe repair

A cracked drain line is a different job. Instead of spraying, it leaks into the slab or a wall cavity only when something drains. We camera-inspect it and repair or replace the damaged length.

Pipe leak repair options compared

The right repair depends on how much of the line is damaged.

OptionBest forTrade-off
Cut out and replace a short sectionOne crack, split or failed fitting on an otherwise sound lineFastest and least disruptive, but nearby pipe may fail later
Replace a longer runSeveral pinholes or brittle pipe along one stretchMore wall or ceiling opened, but fewer future calls
Reroute the lineA pipe in a hard-to-reach or damage-prone spotMore work now, easier access later

When a repeated pattern of leaks suggests the whole system is aging, we will say so and explain the choices.

Broken supply valves and fixture lines

Not every burst is in the wall. A broken angle stop under a sink, a cracked toilet supply line or a failed washing machine hose can flood a room fast. These parts are small, inexpensive and often the real cause.

If you can close the small shutoff under the fixture, do so, then call. We replace the valve or line, test the connection and check the neighboring fixtures for the same wear. For work that goes beyond repair, see our plumbing installation page.

What matters locally

A few Coconut Creek details affect how a burst pipe gets handled.

Two utilities across the city

Homes north of Coconut Creek Parkway are on the city utility and those south are on Margate utility. Tell us which you have so we know where your meter and main shutoff are likely to sit.

Storm season

Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30. After a storm, check for pipe damage near fallen trees, shifted fences and roof leaks before turning the water back on.

Slab homes

Many South Florida homes sit on a concrete slab, so a break under the floor may not show water on the surface right away. A warm patch or a running meter with fixtures off is the clue.

Permits

Whether a repair needs a permit is the city's decision. Ask the Coconut Creek Building Department at 954-973-6750 before larger pipe work.

What affects the cost of burst pipe repair

The price reflects how far the damage reaches, not a flat rate.

Pipe material and size

Copper, PEX and CPVC use different parts and methods.

Access to the break

A visible pipe in a garage is quicker than one inside a finished wall or under a slab.

Length of damaged pipe

A single split costs less than a run of pinholes.

Wall, ceiling or slab opening

Cutting, patching and finishing may be separate trades.

After-hours timing

Night and weekend calls can carry a different rate, stated before we begin.

Insurance often treats a sudden burst that floods a room differently from a slow leak that ran for weeks. Sudden damage may be covered, gradual damage is commonly excluded, and the pipe repair itself may not be. Read your policy and photograph everything before cleanup.

What we do not do

A few jobs sit outside a plumber's scope.

  • We do not dry out walls, remove mold or rebuild drywall. Water mitigation companies and general contractors handle that after the pipe is fixed.
  • We do not work on the utility's side of the meter. A break between the meter and the street is for the water utility.
