Emergency faucet repair in Coconut Creek: when a drip becomes a problem

A faucet repair replaces or reseats the worn parts inside a tap, such as a cartridge, washer, O-ring or valve seat, so water stops where it should. In Coconut Creek, FL, emergency faucet repair usually means a faucet that will not shut off, water spraying from the handle or base, or a supply line that has let go under the sink.

Our line answers 24/7, so a spray at midnight gets the same dispatch as a weekday call. Same-day visits are available when the schedule allows, and the steps below help while you wait.

If a faucet is spraying or will not shut off

Water under pressure can soak a cabinet base and swell the wood in an hour. These four steps slow it down.

1

Close the fixture shutoff

Look under the sink for two small valves, one for hot and one for cold. Turn each clockwise until it stops. Outdoor spigots usually have a valve inside the wall or garage.

2

Use the main valve if those are stuck

Old shutoff valves often seize. If they will not turn, close the main valve at the house and tell us when you call.

3

Empty the cabinet

Pull out cleaners and boxes, set a bucket under any drip and dry the floor so the wood does not soak.

4

Check the lights and outlets

If water has reached an outlet or the light above a bathroom vanity, keep clear and switch that circuit off only if the breaker panel is dry.

If water is coming from the wall or ceiling and not from the faucet itself, see our burst pipe repair page.

Faucet problems in the words homeowners use

People rarely say cartridge or seat. They describe what the tap is doing, and each symptom points to a different part.

It drips after I turn it off

A worn washer, cartridge or valve seat is letting water past.

Water leaks around the handle

An O-ring or packing nut has loosened or worn.

It sprays from the base

A cracked body, a failed seal or a loose spout connection.

Pressure dropped at one tap

Mineral buildup in the aerator or a partly blocked cartridge.

Hot and cold mix wrong

A worn single-handle cartridge no longer separates the two lines.

The handle is stiff or wobbly

Corroded parts inside, or a loose mounting nut under the sink.

Repair the faucet or replace it?

A faucet is usually worth repairing when the finish is sound and the fix is a cartridge, washer or O-ring. These parts cost little and a repair can add years.

Replacement makes more sense when the body is cracked, when parts for an old model are hard to find or when you want a different style or a pull-down spray. If the faucet has leaked many times, a new one often saves repeat visits.

If you are replacing, tell us the number of holes in your sink or counter. That one measurement decides which faucets will fit without drilling.

How a faucet replacement goes

A standard replacement takes a short visit when the shutoff valves still work. Here is the order we follow.

  1. 1

    Shut off and clear the space

    We close the fixture valves, open the old faucet to relieve pressure and clear the cabinet.

  2. 2

    Disconnect and remove

    Supply lines come off, the mounting nuts are loosened and the old faucet is lifted out.

  3. 3

    Clean and set the new one

    The mounting surface is cleaned, the new faucet is seated and the nuts are tightened evenly.

  4. 4

    Connect and test

    New supply lines go on, the aerator is flushed and we test hot, cold and the handle for leaks.

Choosing a faucet that holds up

A few features matter more than the brand name on the box.

Valve type

Ceramic disc cartridges tend to resist wear. Ask what is inside before you choose a model, not only how it looks.

Mounting holes

Count the holes in the sink. One hole, three holes and wall mount all need different faucets.

Finish

Chrome is easy to clean. Brushed and matte finishes hide spots but can show wear faster around handles.

Spout reach and height

A tall gooseneck helps with big pots but can splash in a shallow sink. Check the clearance first.

Why a small drip is worth fixing

A single drip a second adds up, and a hot water tap that drips also burns energy at the water heater. The bill rises slowly, which is why people miss it.

If a faucet is fixed and the meter still moves with every tap off, the leak is somewhere else. Our leak detection service finds it without opening walls, and a leak in the yard line is covered on our water line repair page.

Kitchen faucets often sit near the disposal, so a drip under the sink can come from either one. If the unit is the source, our garbage disposal repair team handles that side.

Outdoor spigots and hose bibs in Coconut Creek

Outdoor faucets in Coconut Creek see sun, rain and sprinkler overspray all year. A hose bib that drips at the stem or leaks inside the wall when the hose is on needs attention, since the wall side is hidden.

Broward County limits when lawns can be watered, so a spigot left running or a leaking hose connection can undo a good schedule. Replace the vacuum breaker or the whole spigot when it will not stop, and avoid leaving hoses attached under pressure.

What affects the cost of faucet repair

A faucet visit can be a ten-minute part swap or a full replacement with new supply lines. These factors set the price.

Repair or replacement

A cartridge or washer is a small job. A new faucet adds the fixture and disposal of the old one.

Faucet type

Pull-down, touchless and wall-mounted models take longer than a standard two-handle tap.

Shutoff and supply lines

Seized valves and brittle lines are often replaced at the same visit.

Cabinet access

A tight sink base or a vanity with little room slows the work.

After-hours visit

Emergency calls are priced differently from daytime repairs, and we tell you the price before work starts.

Insurance often treats sudden damage from a failed supply line differently from the wear that caused a dripping tap. If water reached the cabinet or floor, take photos and ask your insurer what applies.
