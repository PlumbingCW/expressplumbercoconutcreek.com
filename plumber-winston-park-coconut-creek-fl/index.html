Winston Park in Coconut Creek, and a name that appears three times

The name Winston Park refers to three things in Coconut Creek: the neighborhood, the city's Winston Park and Nature Trail at 5201 NW 49th Avenue, and Winston Park Elementary, which opened in 1990. When you call, say that you are in the Winston Park neighborhood and give us your street so we do not send a truck to the school or the park.

Real-estate sites describe the neighborhood as mostly single-family homes built from the late 1980s into the early 2000s. We treat that as a rough guide only, because the city has not published a boundary or a year-built breakdown. For the rest of the city, see our Coconut Creek emergency plumbing page.

Plumbing calls that fit Winston Park houses

Homes from the 1990s are past the point where original fixtures and heaters are new. These are the visits we expect, each starting with a diagnosis.

  • No hot water, a tripped element or a leaking tank, which starts with water heater repair
  • A stain, damp drywall or a bill that jumped, handled with leak detection
  • A slow or backing-up sink, tub or floor drain, which calls for drain cleaning
  • A toilet that runs, leaks at the base or rocks, covered by toilet repair

Before you call about a Winston Park leak

Find your main shutoff first. It is usually at the house wall or in the meter box, and turning it fully off stops most leaks while you wait. Open one faucet afterward to release pressure.

If the water is coming from the street side of the meter, the water utility handles it. We have not confirmed which utility serves each Winston Park street, so your water bill is the reliable answer. For permit questions, the Coconut Creek Building Department is at 954-973-6750.

Check any plumber, including us, on the state's Verify a Licensee page at myfloridalicense.com. Our service areas page lists the other towns we cover.

Winston Park plumbing questions

Both. The city runs the Winston Park and Nature Trail at 5201 NW 49th Avenue, and a residential neighborhood shares the name. Tell us your street when you call so we go to the right place.

Listing sites describe them as built from the late 1980s to the early 2000s, but the city has not published a year-built breakdown. We check each house instead of assuming what is behind the walls.

Age alone does not decide it. A leaking tank means replacement, while a tripped element or a bad thermostat can be repaired. Call and we test it before recommending either water heater repair or a new tank.

Many associations ask for notice before exterior or shared-line work. Their rules are not published, so check with your association before the visit, especially for work outside the house.
